## STRICT VISUAL GUARDRAILS (NO AI SLOP)
1. **NO Terminal / Cyberpunk Clichés:** Never use `font-mono` for section headers, never use double slashes `//`, never use square brackets like `[01]`, and never add pulsing neon dots (`●` or `■`) or pill badges.
2. **Typography Only from PRD:**
   - Headings & Labels: `font-display` (Mona Sans), uppercase, bold/black.
   - Editorial Accents & Numbers: `font-brier` (Brier Serif Italic).
   - Body Copy: `font-sans` (Inter / Manrope).
3. **No Neon Colors:** Never use neon green (`#00FF88`), neon orange (`#FF6B00`), or glowing cyan (`#00d2ff`). Stick strictly to Warm Bone (`#F4F4ED`), Deep Charcoal (`#111112`), and Editorial Cobalt Blue (`#0055FF`).
4. **Alternating Light & Dark Sections:** Maintain the alternating rhythm between `#F4F4ED` (Hero, About, Arsenal, Footer Frame) and `#111112` (Ticker, Work, Journey, Footer Card).
